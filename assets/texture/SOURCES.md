# Texture sources

All source images are Public Domain through The Met Open Access program.

- Border: Sebald Beham, *Frieze with a Horned Male Figure*, 1500–1550, [56.500.78](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/418377).
- Grain: Agostino Veneziano, *Ornamental Panel*, ca. 1514–36, [49.97.180](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/342941).
- Relief: Anonymous, *Ornamental Panel*, ca. 1514–1600, [27.23.87](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/342934).

The images were cropped, mirrored, desaturated, and contrast-adjusted.

`field-symbols.svg` and `diamond-rule.svg` are original vector artwork created for this portfolio.
